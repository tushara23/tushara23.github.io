Commanders Analytics is an independent blog about the Washington Commanders, built on public football data.

## Data

- Play-by-play: [nflverse](https://github.com/nflverse)
- Charting (motion, blitzers, formation): FTN Data, via nflverse
- Pressure, blitz, pass-rush and coverage data: PFF, credited in each post where it's used

Samples are often small, especially early in the season, and posts say so when they are.

Not affiliated with the Washington Commanders or the NFL.
